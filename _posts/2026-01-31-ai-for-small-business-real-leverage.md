## 🚫 What AI Is NOT for Small Businesses

Let’s clear the noise.

AI is not:
- a system that runs your business for you;
- a replacement for your staff;
- a one-click “install and profit” solution;
- automation without thinking.

Most small businesses fail with AI because they try to **replace human judgment instead of amplifying it**.
